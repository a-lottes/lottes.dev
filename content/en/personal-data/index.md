## Hosting

`lottes.dev` and `aspark.lottes.dev` are hosted by Netlify, Inc. (San Francisco, USA). When a page is requested, Netlify processes the technically necessary data – in particular IP address, time, requested address and browser identifier – to deliver the page and keep the service secure. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; my legitimate interest is a secure, working service. See [Netlify's privacy policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/).

## Fonts

All fonts are served by these websites themselves. No connection is made to Google Fonts or any other font service.

## Animation library on aspark.lottes.dev

The animations on `aspark.lottes.dev` use the GSAP library, loaded from cdnjs (Cloudflare, Inc., USA). Cloudflare receives your IP address and browser identifier in the process. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. If the library is blocked, the site keeps working without animations.

## Storage in your browser

`lottes.dev` remembers your chosen colour scheme (light or dark) in your browser's local storage. `aspark.lottes.dev` remembers for the length of the session that the intro has already played. Both stay on your device and are never transmitted.

## Contact by email

If you write to me, I use your details solely to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once they are no longer needed for that.

## External links

Links to GitHub, LinkedIn, X or YouTube are plain links. No data reaches these services until you click one; from then on their privacy policies apply.

## Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection (Art. 15–21 GDPR). You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, for example the Unabhängiges Datenschutzzentrum Saarland.
